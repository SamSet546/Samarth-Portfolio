About

About me

Hey! I'm Samarth, a Mechanical Engineering student at Johns Hopkins University. More than anything, I love basketball. It actually defines the engineer I am today:

  1. I'm always invested in practicing new shooting forms no matter how many shots I miss, just like how I rapidly test new versions of my designs and manufacturing techniques to eventually reach the sweet spot.
  2. I'm conscious about every move I make: dribbling through the legs or behind the back, passing instead of shooting, defending close to the player or backing off. I don't want to make an engineering decision that costs me, which is why I truly enjoy working with software that analyzes mechanical failure and hazards.
  3. Finally, every time I get on the court I track how many more free throws or three pointers I make in a row than the last time. So I always follow the data to see if my system's actually working as intended.

These are reflected in my projects and activities, and I appreciate you taking the time to explore them!
