About me

I'm an engineering student working across materials science, aerospace structures, and data analysis. Most of my work starts the same way: build the thing, instrument it, then find out how far the model was off.

Right now that means corrosion testing of magnesium–aluminum systems at the Weihs Lab, banner deployment and fuselage structures for Design Build Fly, fire-response modeling of wall assemblies, and analytics dashboards for small businesses.
